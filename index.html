 About Nafil Market
Nafil Market is a simple, no-frills service to help you manage small businesses and rental properties. Post listings for shops, stalls, apartments, or equipment rentals. View contact details and manage inquiries the old-fashioned way.
Services
  • Business listings (shops, stalls, services)
  • Rental postings (apartments, rooms, equipment)
  • Contact forwarding (email / phone)
  • Simple management tips and checklists
